KASHMIR OUR ROOTS
ABOUT US

Rooted in Kashmir, Made with Heritage.

The Friends Heritage is a celebration of Kashmir's timeless natural treasures, traditional craftsmanship and authentic flavours.

Since 1947, we've been growing premium Kashmiri saffron in our own fields and carefully sourcing the finest dry fruits from trusted growers around the world.

Our deep roots in Kashmir's saffron-growing tradition, combined with our commitment to quality and sustainability, have made us a trusted name in the industry.

PURE & AUTHENTIC
OUR STORY

A Heritage Built on Trust & Tradition

Over the years, our journey has remained deeply connected to the land, the people and the traditions that make Kashmir special. Every product we select carries a part of that story.

We believe that authentic quality begins at the source, which is why we take great care in selecting products that meet our standards of freshness, purity and natural goodness.

The Friends Heritage is more than a name. It represents friendship, trust and a shared appreciation for the finest things nature has to offer. Our goal is to bring the authentic taste and character of Kashmir directly to your home.

OUR MISSION

Quality You Can Feel & Trust

Our mission is to make premium, authentic natural products accessible while maintaining high standards of quality, freshness and authenticity.

OUR VISION

Sharing Kashmir With The World

We envision The Friends Heritage becoming a trusted name for authentic Kashmiri products, connecting the beauty and heritage of Kashmir with families around the world.
