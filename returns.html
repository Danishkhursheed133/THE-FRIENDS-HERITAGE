Returns & Refunds

Your satisfaction matters to us. We are committed to making your shopping experience simple, transparent, and trustworthy.

OUR COMMITMENT

Your Satisfaction
Matters To Us

At The Friends Heritage, we carefully select, check, and pack every product before it is dispatched.

We want every customer to receive their order safely and in good condition. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us as soon as possible so that we can review the matter and assist you.

RETURN POLICY

When Can You Request A Return?

Returns or replacements may be considered in situations where the product received is damaged, incorrect, or has another genuine issue related to the order.

Damaged Product

If your package arrives visibly damaged or the product is damaged, please contact us promptly with photographs and your order details.

Wrong Product

If you receive a product different from the one you ordered, please contact us so we can review the order and assist with the appropriate solution.

Product Issue

If you believe there is a genuine issue with the product received, please share the details with us as soon as possible.

IMPORTANT

Please Contact Us Before Sending Anything Back

Please do not send a product back without contacting us first. Our team will review your request and provide the appropriate instructions based on your situation.

SIMPLE PROCESS

How To Request A Return

01

Contact Us

Contact our team with your order details and explain the issue.

02

Share Details

If required, share clear photographs or other information related to the issue.

03

Review

We will review the request and determine the appropriate solution.

04

Resolution

Once approved, we will guide you through the replacement or refund process.

REFUND INFORMATION

How Refunds Work

If a refund is approved after reviewing your request, the refund process will be initiated according to the payment method and transaction process used for your order.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider or banking institution.

Requests are reviewed individually.
Approved refunds are processed through the applicable payment method.
Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.
PRODUCT CARE

Product Safety & Quality

Our products are selected and packed with care before dispatch. Because food and consumable products require special handling, return eligibility may depend on the condition of the product and the reason for the request.

For this reason, please contact us before returning any food, saffron, dry fruits, or other consumable product.

BEFORE REQUESTING A RETURN

A Few Important Points

Keep Your Order Details

Please keep your order number, contact information, and purchase details available when contacting us.

Photographs May Be Required

For damaged or incorrect orders, photographs may be requested to help us understand the issue.

Keep The Original Packaging

Where possible, keep the original product and packaging until your return request has been reviewed.

Contact Us Promptly

Please contact us as soon as possible after receiving your order if you notice any problem.

WE ARE HERE TO HELP

Have An Issue With Your Order?

Contact The Friends Heritage with your order details. Our team will be happy to understand the issue and assist you.
