Shipping & Delivery

Carefully packed with care, delivered from our heritage to your doorstep.

OUR DELIVERY PROMISE

From Our Hands
To Your Home

At The Friends Heritage, we believe that quality does not end when an order is placed. Every product is carefully checked, packed, and prepared so that it can reach you safely and in excellent condition.

Whether you order premium saffron, dry fruits, or other carefully selected products, we take every step to make your delivery experience simple, secure, and reliable.

ORDER PROCESSING

Your Order Is Carefully Prepared

Once your order is successfully placed, our team carefully checks the order details and prepares your products for packaging.

We make sure that the selected products, quantity, and delivery information are properly verified before the order is prepared for dispatch.

DELIVERY ACROSS INDIA

Bringing Our Products To You

We provide delivery services across India. Your order is carefully packed and handed over to a reliable courier service for transportation.

Delivery time may vary depending on your location, courier availability, weather conditions, holidays, and other unforeseen circumstances.

SHIPPING INFORMATION

Simple & Transparent Delivery

Shipping charges may vary depending on the delivery location and order details. The applicable delivery charges will be shown clearly during checkout before you place your order.

DELIVERY TIME

When Will Your Order Arrive?

After your order has been processed and dispatched, the courier company will transport your package to the delivery address provided during checkout.

The exact delivery time can vary depending on the destination and courier network. Unexpected delays may occur due to weather, public holidays, transportation issues, or other circumstances beyond our control.

SAFE PACKAGING

Packed With Care

We understand the importance of protecting your products during transportation. Therefore, every order is packed carefully before dispatch.

Our aim is to ensure that your saffron, dry fruits, and other products reach you safely and in the best possible condition.

ORDER TRACKING

Want To Know Where Your Order Is?

If you need an update about your order, please contact us with your order details. Our team will be happy to assist you with the latest available delivery information.
