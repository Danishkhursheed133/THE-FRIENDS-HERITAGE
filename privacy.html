YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Your Information, Your Trust

At The Friends Heritage, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website and purchasing our products.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to keep it secure.

Last Updated: September 2026
01

Information We Collect

When you place an order, contact us, or interact with our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, PIN code, order details, and other information necessary to process and support your order.

02

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to process and deliver your orders, communicate with you, respond to enquiries, and provide customer support.

Information may also be used to improve our website, products, services, and overall customer experience.

03

Order & Delivery Information

When you purchase products from us, certain information is required to complete your order.

Your delivery information may be shared with relevant delivery or courier service providers when necessary to deliver your order.

04

Payment Information

If payment services are provided through third-party payment providers, payment information may be processed directly by those providers.

We do not intend to store sensitive payment credentials such as card passwords or payment PINs on our website.

Third-party payment providers may have their own privacy policies and terms.

05

Communication With You

We may use the contact information you provide to communicate with you about your order, delivery, enquiries, customer service requests, or other necessary website-related matters.

Communication may take place through email, phone, WhatsApp, or other communication methods you provide.

06

Cookies & Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, improve performance, remember preferences, or understand how visitors interact with the website.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information provided to us against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as payment providers, courier services, analytics tools, communication platforms, hosting services, or other external providers.

These third parties may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

09

Sharing Of Information

We do not intend to sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to process orders, deliver products, provide requested services, comply with legal requirements, or protect the rights and security of our business and customers.

10

Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, maintain business records, provide customer support, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

11

Your Choices & Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you.

You may contact us to ask questions, request corrections to inaccurate information, or raise concerns about how your information is being used.

12

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where such consent is required by law.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

13

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

14

Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us.
